# Farecart data: Chicago food locations

Two tiers of places where a Chicago family can get food, plus the scripts that
load them into Supabase. **Nothing here has been phone-verified yet** —
`verified_on` is blank on every row on purpose. See the checklist at the bottom.

| Tier | File | Rows | `source_kind` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curated (West Side, hand-researched Oct 2026) | `locations.csv` | 44: 15 `grocery`, 25 `pantry`, 4 `giveaway` | `curated` |
| Citywide (bulk, citable sources, Oct 2026) | `locations_citywide.csv` | 776: 611 `grocery`, 165 `pantry` | `usda_snap` 609, `plentiful` 145, `other` 18, `osm` 4 |

Live database after seeding: **820 active locations** — 626 grocery, 190 pantry, 4 giveaway.

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `locations.csv` | Curated source of truth. Columns: `name,type,address,neighborhood,lat,lng,hours_text,accepts_ebt,requirements,languages,phone,website,source_url,notes`. `languages` is `;`-separated. Edit by hand only. |
| `locations_citywide.csv` | Generated by `build_citywide.mjs`. Same columns plus `source_kind`. Regenerate, don't hand-edit (hand fixes belong in `locations.csv`, which takes precedence). |
| `giveaway_events.csv` | Dated one-off events linked to a location by exact `location_name`. Columns: `location_name,title,starts_at,ends_at,details,source_url` (ISO 8601 with offset). |
| `fetch_raw.sh` | Downloads the bulk sources into `raw/` (gitignored, untrusted data: only ever parsed, never executed). |
| `build_citywide.mjs` | Reads `raw/`, filters, cleans names, geocodes what has no coordinates, dedupes against each other and against `locations.csv`, assigns `neighborhood` from the City's community-area polygons, writes `locations_citywide.csv`. |
| `seed.mjs` | Loads `locations.csv` then `locations_citywide.csv`, drops any citywide row whose `(name,address)` already appears in the curated tier, geocodes anything still missing coordinates (writing them back to the CSV it came from), validates, and upserts into Supabase via PostgREST. |
| `0002_locations_unique.sql` | `unique (name, address)` on `locations` so the upsert is idempotent. Same file lives in `../supabase/migrations/`. Applied. |
| `../supabase/migrations/0004_source_kind.sql` | Adds `locations.source_kind` (`curated` default, check-constrained to `curated,usda_snap,osm,gcfd,plentiful,other`) and makes `nearby_locations` return it. Applied 2026-10-15. |
| `logos.mjs` | `logoURL(name, website)`: a Google favicon URL (`https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=<host>&sz=128`) for a known Chicago chain (table in the file, checked by eye on 2026-10-15), else for the place's own website host, else null. `seed.mjs` fetches every distinct URL once and nulls out any that comes back as the generic globe or a tiny icon (`--no-logo-check` skips that). 204 of 820 rows have a logo: curated 26, `usda_snap` 176, `plentiful` 1, `other` 1. |
| `../supabase/migrations/0005_logo_url.sql` | Adds `locations.logo_url` and makes `nearby_locations` return it. Applied 2026-10-15. |

## Coverage

Citywide rows by side of the city (from the community area each point falls
in): Far North 139, North 67, Northwest 62, Central 33, West 162 (plus the 44
curated West Side rows), South 85, Southwest 110, Far Southwest 43, Far
Southeast 75. Every one of the 77 community areas has at least one row.

The curated tier covers West Garfield Park, East Garfield Park, Austin, North
Lawndale, Humboldt Park, Near West Side, plus Oak Park, Little Village, West
Town and Cicero; every curated row's `notes` names the nearest CTA rail stop or
bus. Deliberately excluded there (closed): ALDI 3835 W Madison St; Save A Lot
420 S Pulaski Rd; Walmart Neighborhood Market 2551 W Cermak Rd. Not included
because no open-status or schedule could be confirmed: Food Market La Chiquita,
New Mount Pilgrim MBC pantry, Garfield Park Rite to Wellness distributions,
GCFD Producemobile stops, Rush "Food is Medicine" (referral only), Top Box Foods.

## Sources

### Citywide tier (bulk)

- **Grocery — USDA FNS SNAP Retailer Locator** (`source_kind=usda_snap`,
  `accepts_ebt=true`). The ArcGIS feature service behind
  [fns.usda.gov/snap/retailer-locator](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/retailer-locator)
  ([dataset page](https://usda-snap-retailers-usda-fns.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/8b260f9a10b0459aa441ad8588c2251c),
  service `services1.arcgis.com/RLQu0rK7h4kbsBq5/.../snap_retailer_location_data`),
  filtered to `City='CHICAGO' AND State='IL'` (1,734 rows on 2026-10-15). Kept
  store types: Supermarket, Super Store, Grocery Store, Farmers and Markets, and
  Specialty Store only when the name is clearly food (bakery, meat, poultry,
  seafood, produce). The current USDA file has a single "Grocery Store" class
  (no Large/Medium split), so it includes neighborhood grocers of every size.
  Dropped: Convenience Store, Restaurant Meals Program, Other, and any name
  containing gas/liquor/dollar/pharmacy/cellular/mini-mart words. Deduped by
  address. Coordinates are USDA's own. `notes` says "Listed by USDA as a
  SNAP-authorized <type>". Store numbers and corporate suffixes are stripped
  from names ("Jewel Food Store 3376" → "Jewel-Osco"). **Licensing: US federal
  government work, public domain.**
- **Grocery hours — OpenStreetMap** (`shop=supermarket|greengrocer` inside
  Chicago's boundary relation 122604, via Overpass). A USDA row within 80 m of
  an OSM shop with a similar name gets `hours_text` (and website/phone if
  missing) from OSM; its `notes` cites the OSM object. 92 of 611 grocery rows
  got hours this way. Everything else has blank hours.
- **Pantries — Plentiful directory** (`source_kind=plentiful`).
  [directory.plentiful.org/illinois/chicago](https://directory.plentiful.org/illinois/chicago)
  (robots.txt: "Welcome all crawlers"), 175 cards, 164 with a Chicago address;
  each detail page's schema.org block gives address, coordinates, phone, hours
  (only 20 of 175 publish hours) and a description. `source_url` is the
  detail page. `requirements` is filled only when the description mentions
  ID / proof of address / zip / appointment / registration.
- **Pantries — OpenStreetMap** (`source_kind=osm`): `social_facility=food_bank`
  / `soup_kitchen` / `amenity=food_bank` inside Chicago. Only 9 objects exist;
  4 survived dedupe (two "WIC" stores are typed `grocery`; the GCFD warehouse
  is dropped because it does not serve the public). `source_url` is the OSM
  object URL.
- **Pantries — foodpantries.org** (`source_kind=other`):
  [foodpantries.org/ci/il-chicago](https://www.foodpantries.org/ci/il-chicago)
  (robots.txt only blocks `/member/`); 33 schema.org entries, 18 new after
  dedupe. Third-party directory, so hours are left blank and its description is
  truncated into `notes`.
- **Failed / not used:** Greater Chicago Food Depository (`chicagosfoodbank.org`
  returns 403 to scripts; its Vivery map is script-rendered) — no `gcfd` rows
  were produced. Nothing was fetched from GCFD or Vivery.
- **Neighborhoods:** City of Chicago
  [Community Areas](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Facilities-Geographic-Boundaries/Boundaries-Community-Areas/cauq-8yn6)
  (Socrata `igwz-8jzy`), point-in-polygon. Rows inside the bounding box but
  outside every community area are dropped (2 rows).

### Geocoding

US Census Geocoder (`geocoding.geo.census.gov`, benchmark `Public_AR_Current`),
fallback Nominatim with a User-Agent at ≤1 request/s. Both scripts reject any
match outside lat 41.64–42.03 / lng -87.95 to -87.52 or whose street number /
name disagree with the address. `build_citywide.mjs` caches results in
`raw/geocode_cache.json`. USDA and Plentiful rows come with their own
coordinates; only foodpantries.org rows (and any Plentiful page without a
`geo` block) are geocoded.

## Re-running

```sh
# from repo root; needs SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY in ../.env.local
bash data/fetch_raw.sh             # downloads sources into data/raw/ (~4 min, polite rate limits)
node data/build_citywide.mjs       # raw/ -> locations_citywide.csv (uses raw/geocode_cache.json)
node data/seed.mjs                 # curated + citywide -> Supabase (idempotent upsert)
node data/seed.mjs --dry-run       # geocode + validate only, no DB writes
node data/seed.mjs --curated-only  # seed only locations.csv
node data/seed.mjs --no-geocode
```

Idempotent: re-running updates rows in place (`Prefer: resolution=merge-duplicates`
on `(name, address)`). The curated CSV is loaded first and any citywide row with
the same `(name, address)` is skipped, so bulk data never overwrites a curated
row. Renaming a place or changing its address creates a new row; deactivate the
old one in the DB (`is_active = false`) rather than deleting so history is kept.
Rows that disappear from a source are **not** deactivated automatically — do
that by hand after checking. To add a hand-researched place: append a row to
`locations.csv` with `lat,lng` blank and a real `source_url`, run the seed, and
commit the CSV with the coordinates it wrote.

Quick check after seeding (Madison & Pulaski):

```sh
curl -s -X POST "$SUPABASE_URL/rest/v1/rpc/nearby_locations" \
  -H "apikey: $SUPABASE_ANON_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"p_lat":41.8807,"p_lng":-87.7262}'
```

Counts by type and source:

```sh
curl -s "$SUPABASE_URL/rest/v1/locations?select=type,source_kind&limit=5000" -H "apikey: $SUPABASE_ANON_KEY"
```

## Caveats on the citywide tier

- USDA says a store is SNAP-authorized; it does not say it is a good grocery
  store, and its "Grocery Store" class includes small corner groceries. The
  curated tier is the one with human judgement.
- Farmers markets (51 rows) are seasonal; `notes` says so.
- Pantry hours are missing for most Plentiful rows (the directory publishes
  hours for about 1 in 9 listings) and for all foodpantries.org rows.
- Names were cleaned automatically; a few USDA names are still odd
  ("Centrals Finer Foods").

## Human verification checklist

For each row, call the number in `phone` (or visit) and confirm. Then fill
`verified_on` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `verified_by` in the database — the CSVs
intentionally have no `verified_on` column, so verification lives in Supabase.

1. Still open / still running the program?
2. Hours exactly as in `hours_text` (ask about holiday weeks).
3. Pantries: ID / proof of address / zip restrictions / how often you can come /
   appointment needed / what to bring (bags, cart).
4. Groceries: Link/EBT accepted at the register? WIC?
5. Languages spoken at the counter.
6. Correct entrance (several church pantries use a side or basement door).
7. Anything to fix in `notes`.

Curated rows most in need of a call (flagged `UNSURE` in `notes`):

- ALDI 5629 W Fillmore, Food 4 Less 4821 W North, Walmart 4650 W North — hours from third-party listings.
- Pete's Fresh Market (all three) — official page lists addresses but no hours/phones.
- Forty Acres Fresh Market — EBT acceptance not stated on its site.
- Greater Holy Temple COGIC — two sources disagree on Friday hours.
- United for Better Living, Greater Garfield MBC, Healing Temple, LeMoyne Street, William V. Banks (no phone found) — single-source hours.
- Pan de Vida — 2701 vs 2657 S Lawndale.
- El Mercadito — languages inferred, not sourced.
- McCormick YMCA mobile pantry — schedule from search snippets only.
- Blessed Maria Gabriella — Catholic Charities page shows two time blocks.
