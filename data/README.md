# Farecart data: Chicago food locations

Two tiers of places where a Chicago family can get food, plus the scripts that
load them into Supabase. **Nothing here has been phone-verified yet** —
`verified_on` is blank on every row on purpose. See the checklist at the bottom.

| Tier | File | Rows | `source_kind` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curated (West Side, hand-researched Oct 2026) | `locations.csv` | 44: 15 `grocery`, 25 `pantry`, 4 `giveaway` | `curated` |
| Citywide (bulk, citable sources, Oct 2026) | `locations_citywide.csv` | 776: 611 `grocery`, 165 `pantry` | `usda_snap` 609, `plentiful` 145, `other` 18, `osm` 4 |
| Feed America (feedam.org directory, Oct 2026) | `locations_feedam.csv` + `feedam_matches.csv` | 40 new `pantry` rows; 177 existing rows linked by `feedam_id` | `feedam` |

Live database after seeding: **860 active locations** — 626 grocery, 230 pantry, 4 giveaway.
Pantry scheduling demo data (migration 0007) lives in the `pantry_*` tables; see
[Pantry inventory and visit scheduling](#pantry-inventory-and-visit-scheduling) below.

## Sources

### Citywide tier (bulk)

- **Grocery — USDA FNS SNAP Retailer Locator** (`source_kind=usda_snap`,
  `accepts_ebt=true`). The ArcGIS feature service behind
  [fns.usda.gov/snap/retailer-locator](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/retailer-locator)
  ([dataset page](https://usda-snap-retailers-usda-fns.hub.arcgis.com/datasets/8b260f9a10b0459aa441ad8588c2251c),
  service `services1.arcgis.com/RLQu0rK7h4kbsBq5/.../snap_retailer_location_data`),
  filtered to `City='CHICAGO' AND State='IL'` (1,734 rows on 2026-10-15). Kept
  store types: Supermarket, Super Store, Grocery Store, Farmers and Markets, and
  Specialty Store only when the name is clearly food (bakery, meat, poultry,
  seafood, produce). The current USDA file has a single "Grocery Store" class
  (no Large/Medium split), so it includes neighborhood grocers of every size.
  Dropped: Convenience Store, Restaurant Meals Program, Other, and any name
  containing gas/liquor/dollar/pharmacy/cellular/mini-mart words. Deduped by
  address. Coordinates are USDA's own. `notes` says "Listed by USDA as a
  SNAP-authorized <type>". Store numbers and corporate suffixes are stripped
  from names ("Jewel Food Store 3376" → "Jewel-Osco"). **Licensing: US federal
  government work, public domain.**
- **Grocery hours — OpenStreetMap** (`shop=supermarket|greengrocer` inside
  Chicago's boundary relation 122604, via Overpass). A USDA row within 80 m of
  an OSM shop with a similar name gets `hours_text` (and website/phone if
  missing) from OSM; its `notes` cites the OSM object. 92 of 611 grocery rows
  got hours this way. Everything else has blank hours.
- **Pantries — Plentiful directory** (`source_kind=plentiful`).
  [directory.plentiful.org/illinois/chicago](https://directory.plentiful.org/illinois/chicago)
  (robots.txt: "Welcome all crawlers"), 175 cards, 164 with a Chicago address;
  each detail page's schema.org block gives address, coordinates, phone, hours
  (only 20 of 175 publish hours) and a description. `source_url` is the
  detail page. `requirements` is filled only when the description mentions
  ID / proof of address / zip / appointment / registration.
- **Pantries — OpenStreetMap** (`source_kind=osm`): `social_facility=food_bank`
  / `soup_kitchen` / `amenity=food_bank` inside Chicago. Only 9 objects exist;
  4 survived dedupe (two "WIC" stores are typed `grocery`; the GCFD warehouse
  is dropped because it does not serve the public). `source_url` is the OSM
  object URL.
- **Pantries — foodpantries.org** (`source_kind=other`):
  [foodpantries.org/ci/il-chicago](https://www.foodpantries.org/ci/il-chicago)
  (robots.txt only blocks `/member/`); 33 schema.org entries, 18 new after
  dedupe. Third-party directory, so hours are left blank and its description is
  truncated into `notes`.
- **Pantries — Feed America** (`source_kind=feedam`): see the section below.
- **Failed / not used:** Greater Chicago Food Depository (`chicagosfoodbank.org`
  returns 403 to scripts; its Vivery map is script-rendered) — no `gcfd` rows
  were produced. Nothing was fetched from GCFD or Vivery.
- **Neighborhoods:** City of Chicago
  [Community Areas](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Facilities-Geographic-Boundaries/Boundaries-Community-Areas/cauq-8yn6)
  (Socrata `igwz-8jzy`), point-in-polygon. Rows inside the bounding box but
  outside every community area are dropped (2 rows).

### Feed America (feedam.org)

[Feed America](https://feedam.org) (EIN 92-1761881, a 501(c)(3) based in
Houston; not Feeding America) publishes a national food-resource directory
under **CC BY 4.0**. Required attribution, shown wherever its rows or its live
status appear: **"Directory published by Feed America (feedam.org)"**. Each
record also names its own upstream source (`data_source`: `plentiful_org`,
`ampleharvest_org`, `foodpantries_org`, `salvation_army`, `211`, …), which we
repeat in `notes`.

How `feedam.mjs` uses the API (spec at `https://feedam.org/api/openapi.json`):

- Enumerates with `GET /api/resources/bulk?state=IL&type=<t>&limit=1000&page=n`
  for `food_pantry`, `soup_kitchen`, `food_bank`, `mobile_pantry` (5 requests;
  1,886 Illinois rows, 242 with `city=Chicago` inside our bounding box).
  The grid of `/api/resources/nearby?lat&lng` calls was tried first and is not
  needed: bulk is complete and cheaper.
- Fetches `GET /api/resources/{id}` for each Chicago row (242 requests) because
  the bulk export leaves out `requirements_text`, `requires_id`,
  `requires_residency`, `income_limit_fpl`, `requires_appointment`,
  `languages_offered`, `services_offered_json`, `verification_status`.
- Public page per record: `https://feedam.org/resource/{id}` (the
  `feedam_detail_url` the API itself returns); that is each row's `source_url`.
- **Rate limit: 10,000 requests per day per IP.** The script sends
  `User-Agent: Farecart/0.1 (hello@farecart.org)`, waits ≥550 ms between
  requests (≤2/s), backs off 30 s on HTTP 429, and stops at 9,000. A full run
  is ~250 requests; re-runs with `--no-fetch` use the cache in `raw/feedam/`.
- `GET /api/resources/{id}/status` (phone validity, `hours_status`,
  `confidence`, `days_since_verified`) is **not** stored; the API route
  `GET /pantry/:id/availability` and the iOS app call it live for rows with a
  `feedam_id` and cache it 10 minutes.

Field mapping: `hours_json` → `hours_text` ("Mon-Fri 10am-2pm");
`requirements_text` → `requirements` (short area names become
"Service area: West Side"); `requires_id` → `requires_id` (true/false/blank);
`requires_residency`, `income_limit_fpl`, `requires_appointment` → appended to
`requirements`; `languages_offered` → `languages` (English dropped);
`services_offered_json` and `organization` → `notes`; `website` only when it is
the pantry's own site, not a directory page; `resource_type` → our `pantry`
type with the kind ("soup kitchen (hot meals)") in `notes`. `accepts_walkins`
is 1 on nearly every record and is ignored. 211-sourced rows carry coordinates
rounded to a ~0.005° grid, so those 9 are re-geocoded with the Census geocoder
(cached in `raw/geocode_cache.json`); one could not be and was dropped.

Dedupe, in order: same normalised address as any pantry/giveaway row we
already have; within 60 m with a similar name; same house number + street name
with a similar name (catches "Jackson Ave" vs "Jackson Blvd"). Later Feed
America rows that match an already-matched row are dropped (Feed America
itself aggregates Plentiful, AmpleHarvest and foodpantries.org, so duplicates
are common). New rows are also deduped against each other within 150 m.

Result on 2026-10-16: 242 Chicago rows (236 `food_pantry`, 3 `soup_kitchen`,
3 `food_bank`, 0 `mobile_pantry`) → **177 matched** existing rows (173 by
address, 3 by distance+name, 1 by street+name; 137 Plentiful, 21 curated, 18
foodpantries.org, 1 OSM), **40 new** rows (39 `food_pantry`, 1 `soup_kitchen`;
7 with hours, 7 with requirements, 0 with `requires_id`), 21 second records
for already-matched rows, 2 in-set duplicates, 1 outside the community areas,
1 without usable coordinates. No Feed America record in Chicago sets
`requires_id`, `requires_residency` or `income_limit_fpl`.

Known leftovers: "Care for Real" (5339 N Sheridan, AmpleHarvest) and "Care for
Real Food Pantry" (5341 N Sheridan, Plentiful) are the same organisation at
neighbouring addresses more than 150 m apart in the sources' coordinates; the
two "Irving Park Community Food Pantry" rows are at different addresses in
their sources. Both pairs are kept for a human to resolve.

### Pantry metadata (`pantry_model`, `requires_snap`, `requires_id`)

Migration 0007 adds three nullable facts to `locations`, and `nearby_locations`
returns them. The app shows "Most pantries don't require SNAP or ID" unless a
row says otherwise, so **blank is the safe value**: nothing here is inferred.

- `pantry_model`: `client_choice` (you pick items, grocery-style),
  `pre_packed` (a prepared bag/box) or `both`. Filled only where the captured
  text says so: Breakthrough Fresh Market ("client-choice shopping"). GCFD and
  Plentiful pages for the other 24 curated pantries describe them only as
  "food pantry and grocery distribution", which does not say which model.
- `requires_snap`: false by default (DB default); no source lists a SNAP
  requirement.
- `requires_id`: true only for Marillac St. Vincent ("bring proof of name and
  current address"); "may ask name and zip" is not an ID requirement and stays
  blank; Legler's "no ID or zip requirement stated" means the source did not
  say, so it stays blank. Feed America's `requires_id` flag is mapped when
  present (none of the Chicago records set it).

### Geocoding

US Census Geocoder (`geocoding.geo.census.gov`, benchmark `Public_AR_Current`),
fallback Nominatim with a User-Agent at ≤1 request/s. Both scripts reject any
match outside lat 41.64–42.03 / lng -87.95 to -87.52 or whose street number /
name disagree with the address. `build_citywide.mjs` caches results in
`raw/geocode_cache.json`. USDA and Plentiful rows come with their own
coordinates; only foodpantries.org rows (and any Plentiful page without a
`geo` block) are geocoded.

## Re-running

```sh
# from repo root; needs SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY in ../.env.local
bash data/fetch_raw.sh             # downloads sources into data/raw/ (~4 min, polite rate limits)
node data/build_citywide.mjs       # raw/ -> locations_citywide.csv (uses raw/geocode_cache.json)
node data/seed.mjs                 # curated + citywide -> Supabase (idempotent upsert)
node data/seed.mjs --dry-run       # geocode + validate only, no DB writes
node data/seed.mjs --curated-only  # seed only locations.csv
node data/seed.mjs --no-geocode
node data/feedam.mjs [--no-fetch] [--refresh]   # Feed America -> locations_feedam.csv + feedam_matches.csv (~250 polite requests)
node data/demo_pantry.mjs [--reset] [--rotate]  # demo slots / inventory / staff codes for 12 West Side pantries
```

Idempotent: re-running updates rows in place (`Prefer: resolution=merge-duplicates`
on `(name, address)`). The curated CSV is loaded first, then citywide, then Feed
America; any later row with the same `(name, address)` is skipped, so bulk data
never overwrites a curated row. `feedam_matches.csv` is applied last and only
touches `feedam_id`. Renaming a place or changing its address creates a new row; deactivate the
old one in the DB (`is_active = false`) rather than deleting so history is kept.
Rows that disappear from a source are **not** deactivated automatically — do
that by hand after checking. To add a hand-researched place: append a row to
`locations.csv` with `lat,lng` blank and a real `source_url`, run the seed, and
commit the CSV with the coordinates it wrote.

Quick check after seeding (Madison & Pulaski):

```sh
curl -s -X POST "$SUPABASE_URL/rest/v1/rpc/nearby_locations" \
  -H "apikey: $SUPABASE_ANON_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"p_lat":41.8807,"p_lng":-87.7262}'
```

Counts by type and source:

```sh
curl -s "$SUPABASE_URL/rest/v1/locations?select=type,source_kind&limit=5000" -H "apikey: $SUPABASE_ANON_KEY"
```

## Pantry inventory and visit scheduling

Migration `0007_pantry_scheduling.sql` (applied 2026-10-16) implements the
contract in the root README: `pantry_inventory` (one row per
location+category, 24 h expiry), `pantry_slots` (`remaining = capacity -
booked`), `pantry_visits` (6-char confirmation code from
`ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789`, `cancel_token` uuid) and
`pantry_staff_codes`. Anon can `select` inventory and slots; visits and staff
codes are not readable at all (no policy, privileges revoked) and are reached
only through the `security definer` functions: `pantry_availability`,
`book_visit`, `cancel_visit`, `my_visits`, `staff_login`,
`staff_set_inventory`, `staff_upsert_slot`, `staff_delete_slot`,
`staff_bookings`, `staff_mark_arrived`. Errors come back from PostgREST as
HTTP 400 with `{"code":"P0001","message":"slot_full"}`; messages used:
`slot_full`, `slot_past`, `slot_not_found`, `already_booked`, `bad_device`,
`bad_party_size`, `visit_not_found`, `already_arrived`, `visit_cancelled`,
`bad_code`, `bad_category`, `bad_level`, `bad_times`, `bad_capacity`,
`capacity_below_booked`. `booked` counts bookings (one per device per slot),
not `party_size`; `book_visit` takes a row lock on the slot so two phones
cannot both get the last place. A slot is bookable until it ends.
`staff_delete_slot` deletes the slot and its visits (the count comes back as
`cancelled_visits`). `my_visits` lists upcoming visits first (soonest first),
then the past 30 days (most recent first).

### Demo data and staff codes

`node data/demo_pantry.mjs` creates **demo** slots, inventory and staff codes
for 12 curated West Side pantries (Legler, Keystone, Above and Beyond,
Breakthrough, Marillac, Midwest Corps, Harmony, St. Martin de Porres, Circle
Urban, The Ark, Mission of Our Lady of the Angels, Pan de Vida). Slots are
30 minutes, capacity 8–15, for the next 14 days inside each pantry's
`hours_text` (all 12 parse; weekday 9–11am would be the fallback; hot-meal
hours are skipped), `created_by='demo'`. Inventory covers 5–7 categories per
pantry with mixed levels and `source='demo'`, `reported_at` = run time, so it
**expires 24 h after the last run** — re-run the script before a demo. Run on
2026-10-16: 292 slots, 75 inventory rows, 12 codes.

Idempotent: existing slots with the same `(location, starts_at)` are kept
(bookings survive), inventory is upserted, codes are reused from
`demo_staff_codes.csv`. Flags: `--reset` deletes unbooked demo slots first;
`--rotate` issues new codes; `--dry-run` prints the plan.

**Staff codes are in `data/demo_staff_codes.csv`** (committed; demo only).
They unlock the staff functions for those 12 pantries in this test database.
To rotate: `node data/demo_pantry.mjs --rotate`, commit the new CSV, and tell
whoever is testing. To revoke one pantry's code without a script:
`delete from pantry_staff_codes where location_id = '<id>'`. Before any real
pantry is onboarded, delete the demo rows
(`delete from pantry_staff_codes where contact_name = 'Demo staff'`) and issue
real codes out of band — never commit those.

## Caveats on the citywide tier

- USDA says a store is SNAP-authorized; it does not say it is a good grocery
  store, and its "Grocery Store" class includes small corner groceries. The
  curated tier is the one with human judgement.
- Farmers markets (51 rows) are seasonal; `notes` says so.
- Pantry hours are missing for most Plentiful rows (the directory publishes
  hours for about 1 in 9 listings) and for all foodpantries.org rows.
- Names were cleaned automatically; a few USDA names are still odd
  ("Centrals Finer Foods").

## Human verification checklist

For each row, call the number in `phone` (or visit) and confirm. Then fill
`verified_on` (YYYY-MM-DD) and `verified_by` in the database — the CSVs
intentionally have no `verified_on` column, so verification lives in Supabase.

1. Still open / still running the program?
2. Hours exactly as in `hours_text` (ask about holiday weeks).
3. Pantries: ID / proof of address / zip restrictions / how often you can come /
   appointment needed / what to bring (bags, cart).
4. Groceries: Link/EBT accepted at the register? WIC?
5. Languages spoken at the counter.
6. Correct entrance (several church pantries use a side or basement door).
7. Anything to fix in `notes`.

Curated rows most in need of a call (flagged `UNSURE` in `notes`):

- ALDI 5629 W Fillmore, Food 4 Less 4821 W North, Walmart 4650 W North — hours from third-party listings.
- Pete's Fresh Market (all three) — official page lists addresses but no hours/phones.
- Forty Acres Fresh Market — EBT acceptance not stated on its site.
- Greater Holy Temple COGIC — two sources disagree on Friday hours.
- United for Better Living, Greater Garfield MBC, Healing Temple, LeMoyne Street, William V. Banks (no phone found) — single-source hours.
- Pan de Vida — 2701 vs 2657 S Lawndale.
- El Mercadito — languages inferred, not sourced.
- McCormick YMCA mobile pantry — schedule from search snippets only.
- Blessed Maria Gabriella — Catholic Charities page shows two time blocks.

312-655-7700; Chicago DFSS / 311 for city resources.
